## Countdown voice
The spoken "3, 2, 1" + horn recording (`sounds/countdown_start.wav`) is **not** included in this repository.
Drop your own recording there (the horn should begin exactly 3.00 s in) to enable "Spoken 3-2-1".
Without it, the app falls back to a silent countdown followed by the normal start horn.
